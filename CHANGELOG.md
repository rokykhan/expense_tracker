# Changelog

## Phase 5

- Notes can be added, edited, deleted, pinned, and searched.
- Tasks have a due day, a reminder, and filters for today, upcoming, and done.
- Finishing or deleting a task cancels its reminder. The reminder uses a normal date trigger and does not ask for an exact alarm.

## Phase 4

- Reports can show a category pie and a daily bar chart. The amounts match the month totals.
- A yearly view lists all twelve months and charts the months that had spending.
- A custom view filters by dates, accounts, categories, and kinds, and exports those rows as CSV.

## Phase 3

- The budget tab shows an overall monthly budget and one budget per expense category, with yellow and red progress.
- Savings goals accept deposits and withdrawals and show a completed state.
- Debts and receivables track partial payments, a settled state, and overdue items.

## Phase 2

- Home has one-tap shortcuts. A manage screen adds, edits, and reorders them.
- Recurring rules can repeat daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly, with an optional end date.
- Opening the app, or bringing it back, inserts each missed run once.

## Phase 1

- Added account, category, and transaction screens, with archive, icon and color choices, and delete plus undo.
- Home now shows the total balance, this month's income, expense, and savings, and recent entries.
- Reports show the monthly totals and category percentage bars.
- Profile name and the ৳ symbol are stored in settings.

## Phase 0

- Built the empty Hisabkhata shell inside the Expo starter.
- Added the Bangla font, accent color, four tabs, and the SQLite schema with seed data.
